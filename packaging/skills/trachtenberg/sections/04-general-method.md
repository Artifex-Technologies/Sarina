# Any two numbers: the two-finger method

Each digit of the answer, from the right, is: the units digits of the digit pairs that land on that place, plus the tens digits of
the pairs that land one place to the right, plus the carry. For 23 × 47: place 1 is the units of 3×7 (1); place 2 is the units of
3×4 and 2×7 (2 and 4) plus the tens of 3×7 (2), 8; place 3 is the units of 2×4 (8) plus the tens of 3×4 and 2×7 (1 and 1), 10,
write 0 carry 1; place 4 is the tens of 2×4 (0) plus the carry, 1: 1081. "Two fingers" because the learner points at the pair being
used in each number. It needs the single-digit times table (units and tens of a product), which is why it comes after the rules.
